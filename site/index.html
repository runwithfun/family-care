The simulator demo

One day with
Aoife and Maya

On November 23, eight-week-old Maya has a morning well-baby visit. Her mother, Aoife, has a recovery follow-up that afternoon. The demo uses a fictional family, a synthetic profile photo, and sample clinic documents.

Today & Records

From the day plan to the original record

Today also reminds Aoife to request Maya’s immunization record. In Records, she can open the original sample PDF and leave any unconfirmed details for review.

Assistant

A question to bring to the visit

Aoife can read the original clinic note, then choose Add to Today. Her recovery question becomes a task marked For you.

Growing up

A little time to play

In Growing up, Aoife can save a note for Maya’s next visit. She can also open Maya’s Supervised tummy time card and save it as an A4 or US Letter PDF.

On device

Family records stay on the device

The demo saves confirmed follow-ups locally and runs on the phone without a backend, remote model, or analytics service. GitHub Pages serves this static site.
