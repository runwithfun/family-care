Two visits on one day

Maya has a well-baby visit in the morning. Her mother, Aoife, has a recovery follow-up that afternoon. Family Care gives Aoife a place to keep both visits and prepare a question about her own recovery.

  • A shared day plan See the time and person for each visit or task, then open the item you need.
  • The original record Open the clinic’s sample PDF and keep a missing field unresolved for review.
  • A question to bring Check the visit note in Assistant before adding a follow-up to Today.

From a visit note to Today

In these iPhone demo screens, see the fictional family’s day plan, their original sample record, and the follow-up Aoife can confirm from a prepared visit note. See the demo verification checklist.

The demo runs on the device

The app opens a sample day and runs without a backend, remote model, or analytics service. GitHub Pages hosts this static project site.

  • Sample identities and clinic notes
  • Confirmed follow-ups saved on the device
  • Immunization references shown separately from family records

Run the demo on a simulator

The shared UI uses Kotlin and Compose Multiplatform. Clone the repository, use JDK 17, and follow the iOS or Android steps in the README.
